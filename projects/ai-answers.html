Who Stays When AI Answers? Core-Periphery Resilience in Online Knowledge Communities

Li, L., Zhang, R., Zeng, S., and Shrestha, Y. R. Accepted at ICIS 2026

Research OverviewHomepage

Research Motivation

Generative AI can answer questions instantly, raising a central question for online knowledge communities: which contributors continue participating when AI can provide an alternative source of answers?

Research Questions

  • How did ChatGPT's release reshape participation and contribution?
  • How do these changes differ between core and peripheral community members?

Method

We study 52-week panel data covering 558,980 users across Stack Overflow and Mathematics Stack Exchange. The analysis distinguishes core and peripheral users and examines changes in participation and contribution behavior.

Key Findings

Peripheral users experienced sharper participation declines, while active peripheral users showed larger increases in average contribution length than core users.
