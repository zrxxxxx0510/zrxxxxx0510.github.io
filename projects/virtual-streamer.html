Is More Human-Like Better? The Impact of Virtual Streamer Language Naturalness on User Engagement

Zhang, R., Fang, J., Han, L., and Liao, M. TFSC 2026

PaperHomepage

Research Motivation

As virtual streamers became increasingly common in livestreaming commerce, we asked whether making their language sound more human would always improve user engagement.

Research Questions

  • How does text naturalness affect user engagement?
  • How does voice naturalness affect user engagement?
  • How does anthropomorphic appearance shape these relationships?

Method

We analyzed 394 real-world virtual-streamer livestreaming events from Taobao Live, totaling approximately 3,709 hours. We combined large language models, deep learning, acoustic feature extraction, and econometric analysis to measure text and voice naturalness and relate them to behavioral engagement.

Key Findings

  • Text naturalness is negatively associated with user engagement.
  • Voice naturalness is positively associated with user engagement.
  • Anthropomorphic appearance weakens the negative text effect and strengthens the positive voice effect.
